Our Process

How We Build Your
Custom Pool

Building a pool involves much more than digging a hole and adding water. Every phase plays an important role in the pool’s safety, strength, appearance and long-term performance.

1

Design & Planning

We begin by discussing your vision, available space, preferred features and budget. Our team then develops a pool design that complements your home and creates a functional outdoor living space.

Why It Matters

Careful planning helps prevent costly changes and ensures the size, shape, depth, decking and features work together properly.

2

Engineering & Permitting

Construction plans are prepared and, when required, reviewed by an engineer. We also coordinate the necessary permits and approvals before construction begins.

Why It Matters

Engineering and permitting help ensure the pool complies with structural, electrical, plumbing and local building requirements.

3

Pool Layout

The shape and location of the pool are marked on the property so you can see how the finished project will fit within your backyard.

Why It Matters

The layout confirms the pool’s position, dimensions, setbacks and relationship to the home before excavation begins.

4

Excavation

Our team excavates the pool according to the approved dimensions, depths and design.

Why It Matters

Accurate excavation creates the foundation for the pool’s structure and ensures the depths, steps, benches and other features are formed correctly.

5

Steel Reinforcement

A reinforced steel framework is installed throughout the pool according to the construction and engineering plans.

Why It Matters

The steel framework provides structural strength and helps the pool shell withstand soil movement, water pressure and long-term use.

6

Rough Plumbing

The pool’s circulation lines, drains, skimmers, returns, water features and equipment connections are installed and pressure-tested.

Why It Matters

Proper plumbing provides efficient circulation and filtration while pressure testing helps identify leaks before the lines are covered.

7

Electrical & Bonding

Electrical conduits, bonding and required connections are installed for the pool equipment, lights, automation and accessories.

Why It Matters

Correct bonding and electrical work are essential for safety, equipment operation and code compliance.

8

Gunite or Shotcrete Application

Concrete is pneumatically applied over the steel framework to create the pool’s permanent structural shell.

Why It Matters

This phase creates the strength, shape and structural integrity of the pool. Proper application and curing are critical to long-term performance.

9

Curing

The new pool shell is allowed to cure properly before the finishing phases continue.

Why It Matters

Curing allows the concrete to develop strength and helps create a durable shell designed to last for decades.

10

Tile, Coping & Water Features

Waterline tile, coping, raised walls, spas, waterfalls, sheers and other architectural details are installed.

Why It Matters

These finishes protect the pool’s edges and waterline while establishing the overall style and character of the project.

11

Equipment Installation

The pump, filter, sanitizer, heater, automation system and other selected equipment are installed and connected.

Why It Matters

Properly sized and installed equipment keeps the water circulating, filtered, sanitized and comfortable while improving energy efficiency.

12

Decking & Outdoor Living Features

The surrounding pool deck is constructed using pavers, travertine, concrete or another selected finish. Additional features such as steps, retaining walls and patios are completed during this phase.

Why It Matters

A properly constructed deck provides safe access, manages drainage and connects the pool to the rest of your outdoor living space.

13

Interior Finish

The selected plaster, quartz or pebble finish is applied to the inside of the pool.

Why It Matters

The interior finish waterproofs and protects the pool shell while determining the final water color and underwater appearance.

14

Pool Fill & Startup

The pool is filled with water, the equipment is activated and the water chemistry is carefully balanced.

Why It Matters

A professional startup protects the new interior finish, verifies proper equipment operation and prepares the pool for safe use.

15

Final Walkthrough

We inspect the completed project and show you how to operate and care for your new pool and equipment.

Why It Matters

The final walkthrough ensures everything is working correctly and gives you the information needed to enjoy and maintain your investment confidently.

From Concept to Completion

At Texas Pools & Pavers, every phase is carefully coordinated to deliver a pool that is beautiful, functional and built for long-term enjoyment.
