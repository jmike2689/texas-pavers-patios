The Build Process

Transparency at every phase. Here is how we take your backyard from concept to completion.

1

Design & Permitting

Our in-house team drafts a custom 3D design and handles all necessary city permits.

2

Excavation

We carefully shape the terrain, excavating the exact footprint of your new pool.

3

Steel & Plumbing

A highly engineered steel rebar cage is tied, and all hydraulic plumbing lines are laid.

4

Gunite & Hardscaping

The concrete shell is shot, and we begin installing custom coping, tile, and paver decking.

5

Plaster & Startup

The final interior finish is applied, the pool is filled, and equipment is calibrated.
